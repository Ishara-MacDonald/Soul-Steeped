# Soul Steeped

Playable on [itch.io](https://ika-bits.itch.io/soul-steeped)

Serve tea to restore balance.

You're a local brewer with a small superpower: you can view the state of someone's soul!

Teas made out of different herbs give people a boost to certain needs. Serve your customers the right tea to balance the state of their soul. Each time the customer drinks their tea, their need gets a boost.

It's up to the player to find out which flavours restore which need, and to restore the balance of the customer's coming by.
